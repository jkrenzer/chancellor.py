Version 1.0. Referenced by the git tag `cla-v1.0`.

This agreement is between you and Jörn Krenzer, the maintainer of Chancellor.

Chancellor is free software and is meant to stay free software for good. Free software licences can
still turn out to stand in the way of legitimate free software use: a court ruling, a change in the
law or an incompatibility between two free licences can, for example, stop the Project from being
used as a module in another free software project. This agreement exists so that the Maintainer can
resolve such a problem by making the Project available under a further free software licence,
without having to find every contributor first.

The agreement therefore grants rights to everyone, not to the Maintainer personally. The Maintainer
only gains the power to name further free software licences, and can never make your work
proprietary. You keep the copyright in your contributions.
