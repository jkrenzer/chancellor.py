# Chancellor Entity Contributor Licence Agreement

Version 1.0. Referenced by the git tag `cla-v1.0`.

This agreement is between the legal entity named in the signature block ("the Entity") and Jörn
Krenzer. It covers contributions made by people working for the Entity. Except where this agreement
says otherwise, it takes over the terms of the Chancellor Individual Contributor Licence Agreement
(`CLA.md`) in the same version.

## 1. Incorporation of the individual agreement

1.1. Sections 1 to 7 and 9 to 11 of `CLA.md` apply to the Entity as if it were "You", with these
changes:

1. "Contribution" means any Contribution, as defined in `CLA.md`, submitted by an Authorised
   Contributor (section 2), in which the Entity holds rights.
2. Section 6.1 item 3 of `CLA.md` does not apply. Instead, the Entity confirms that it holds, or has
   obtained, the rights needed to grant the licences for the work of its Authorised Contributors.
3. Section 6.3 of `CLA.md` applies with the addition that the Entity's liability is limited to
   intent and gross negligence also for its employees and agents.

1.2. Section 8 of `CLA.md` is replaced by section 4 of this agreement.

## 2. Authorised Contributors

2.1. "Authorised Contributors" are the people the Entity names to the Maintainer as contributing on
its behalf, identified by their GitHub login.

2.2. The Entity names them in the signed agreement and may change the list at any time by written
notice (email is sufficient) from a person entitled to represent it. A removal takes effect for
Contributions submitted after the Maintainer receives the notice.

2.3. Each Authorised Contributor also accepts `CLA.md` for any rights they personally hold in their
Contributions, for example moral rights or work done outside their employment.

## 3. Signing

3.1. The Entity signs this agreement through a person entitled to represent it. It sends the signed
copy as a PDF to the Maintainer at the address in section 11 of `CLA.md`.

3.2. The Maintainer then commits a record `cla/entities/<entity-slug>.md` with the Entity's name,
the agreement version, the date it took effect and the list of Authorised Contributors.

## 4. Records and personal data

4.1. The signed agreement, including the signatory's name and contact details, is kept by the
Maintainer and is not published.

4.2. The public record names the Entity and its Authorised Contributors' GitHub logins. The Entity
informs its Authorised Contributors about this record.

4.3. Section 8.3 of `CLA.md` applies to the personal data in the public record and in the signed
agreement.
